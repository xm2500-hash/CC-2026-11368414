A Year in One Minute

I chose swallow migration because swallows fly south in winter and north in spring. This was a little story I learned when I was learning to write as a child, and it left a deep impression on me. I used this story to express time, imagining one minute as one year and having a swallow travel between the north and south through the four seasons.

On the left are white walls, dark tiled roofs, and distant mountains in Jiangnan, China (distinctive architecture of southern China). On the right are the red walls and golden roofs of Beijing’s Forbidden City (distinctive architecture of northern China). I used two identical square Chinese window frames and a branch stretching across both sides to connect these two places because I think birds rest on branches.

In spring, the flower buds gradually grow, the snow in Beijing begins to melt, and the swallow flies north. In summer, the flowers bloom, it starts raining in the south, and the swallow stays in Beijing. In autumn, the flowers turn yellow and shrink, and the swallow returns to Jiangnan. In winter, the flowers disappear, and it starts snowing in Beijing. I really, really like my idea, and I think I will continue working on this assignment. It looks like an illustration from my elementary school Chinese textbook!

In the animation, I use second() to control the four seasons, the flowers, and the swallow’s movement, millis() to control the continuous falling of rain and snow, and minute() to make the flowers slightly different in size in different “years.”
